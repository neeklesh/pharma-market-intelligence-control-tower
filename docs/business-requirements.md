# Business Requirements

## 1. Business Problem
Pharmaceutical market intelligence information is spread across multiple sources, making it difficult to efficiently analyse market opportunities, competitor activities, clinical developments, regulatory changes and commercial trends across APAC markets.

## 2. Business Objective
Develop a market intelligence and commercial analytics platform that consolidates publicly available information and analytical datasets to support evidence-based strategic decision-making.

## 3. Geographic Scope
- India
- Singapore
- Malaysia
- Thailand
- China
- Japan

## 4. Therapeutic Scope
- Type 2 Diabetes
- Obesity
- GLP-1 therapies

## 5. Business Requirements

### BR-01: Market Intelligence
The platform should support analysis of market attractiveness across selected countries.

### BR-02: Competitive Intelligence
The platform should maintain structured information about pharmaceutical companies, products and competitive developments.

### BR-03: Clinical Pipeline
The platform should monitor clinical trials and pipeline developments.

### BR-04: Regulatory Intelligence
The platform should track publicly available regulatory developments.

### BR-05: Pricing and Market Access
The platform should support analysis of publicly available pricing and market access information.

### BR-06: Commercial Analytics
The platform should support territory, HCP segmentation, sales performance and commercial planning analysis using synthetic datasets where necessary.

### BR-07: Forecasting
The platform should support scenario-based forecasting using documented assumptions.

### BR-08: AI Intelligence Assistant
The platform should assist in summarising market developments while retaining source references.

### BR-09: Dashboarding
The platform should provide interactive visualisations for market and commercial analysis.

### BR-10: Data Quality
The platform should identify missing, duplicate and invalid records.

## 6. Constraints
- Budget: Zero-cost tools and publicly accessible data wherever possible.
- Data availability: Public data and synthetic data.
- Time: Maximum 1–2 hours per day.
- Geography: Six selected APAC markets.

## 7. Success Criteria
- Data sources integrated and documented.
- SQL-based analytical queries implemented.
- Python-based data processing implemented.
- Market and competitive analyses completed.
- Forecasting models developed.
- Interactive dashboards created.
- AI-assisted summaries tested.
- Project documented and published on GitHub.
