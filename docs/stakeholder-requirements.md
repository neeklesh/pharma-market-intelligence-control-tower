# Stakeholder Requirements

## 1. Purpose
Document the stakeholder needs, business expectations and analytical requirements for the Pharma Market Intelligence & Commercial Strategy Control Tower.

This document represents simulated stakeholder workshop findings for an independent portfolio project.

## 2. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary Responsibilities | Information Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Regional Sales Head | Sales strategy and execution | Market opportunities and territory performance |
| Marketing Head | Brand and marketing strategy | Competition, market trends and therapeutic developments |
| Regional Strategy Manager | Strategic planning | Market attractiveness and future opportunities |
| Commercial Excellence Lead | Sales force effectiveness | HCP segmentation, territory analytics and resource allocation |
| Market Intelligence Manager | Market research and intelligence | Competitive, pipeline and regulatory developments |
| Regional Leadership | Strategic decision-making | Executive insights, forecasts and risks |

## 3. Stakeholder Needs

### Market Intelligence
- Compare market opportunities across six APAC markets.
- Evaluate disease burden and relevant healthcare indicators.
- Assess market attractiveness using documented criteria.
- Support country prioritisation.

### Competitive Intelligence
- Monitor relevant pharmaceutical companies.
- Track products, indications and competitive developments.
- Monitor clinical pipeline and regulatory developments.
- Identify potential competitive threats and opportunities.

### Clinical Pipeline Intelligence
- Track clinical trials.
- Analyse clinical development stages.
- Monitor trial status and relevant dates.
- Identify potential future market developments.

### Regulatory Intelligence
- Monitor regulatory developments across selected countries.
- Track approvals and indication expansions.
- Identify relevant safety and regulatory announcements.

### Pricing and Market Access
- Collect public pricing information where available.
- Analyse affordability indicators.
- Monitor reimbursement and market access developments.

### Commercial Analytics
- Analyse simulated sales performance.
- Evaluate synthetic HCP segmentation.
- Analyse territory performance.
- Evaluate call-plan achievement and HCP coverage.
- Support commercial resource allocation analysis.

### Forecasting
- Support 3–5 year forecasting.
- Develop base, optimistic and pessimistic scenarios.
- Enable assumption-driven analysis.
- Document forecast limitations.

### Dashboard and Platform
- Provide interactive dashboards.
- Support filtering by relevant dimensions.
- Maintain source traceability.
- Provide structured summaries of market developments.
- Support data quality validation.

## 4. Key Business Decisions Supported

1. Which APAC markets should be prioritised?
2. Which competitive developments require attention?
3. Which clinical or regulatory developments may affect the market?
4. Which market segments represent potential opportunities?
5. How should simulated commercial resources be prioritised?
6. How may markets evolve over the next 3–5 years?

## 5. Assumptions

- The project covers India, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, China and Japan.
- The therapeutic focus is diabetes, obesity and GLP-1 therapies.
- Publicly available data will be used.
- Synthetic datasets will be used for commercial analytics.
- Forecasts are analytical scenarios, not actual company forecasts.
- Stakeholder responses are simulated for learning and portfolio demonstration.

## 6. Constraints

- Zero-cost tools and free data sources wherever feasible.
- Maximum 1–2 hours per day.
- Data availability may differ by country.
- Source quality and comparability may vary.
- Analytical assumptions and limitations must be documented.
